The Cheat Sheet: Axes-level vs. Figure-level

To use Seaborn professionally, you need to know which functions play nicely with Matplotlib's ax and which ones act as standalone managers.

1. Axes-Level Functions (The Polite Workers) : 

    These draw specific, single charts. They always accept the ax=ax parameter so you can place them inside complex Matplotlib layouts.

    Examples :-

       - sns.lineplot()

       - sns.scatterplot()

       - sns.histplot()

       - sns.boxplot()

       - sns.barplot()

2. Figure-Level Functions (The Control Freaks) :

    These are umbrella functions designed to create multi-plot grids automatically (using FacetGrid). They do not accept ax=ax. 
    You must let them manage the whole figure. 
    
    Examples :-

        - sns.relplot() (The umbrella for lineplot and scatterplot)

        - sns.displot() (The umbrella for histplot and kdeplot)

        - sns.catplot() (The umbrella for boxplot, barplot, violinplot)
